1. Why I Chose ACT

Posted on October 2026 | By Ram Malabanan

I chose Associate in Computer Technology because I love computers. When I was younger, I always wondered how games and websites were made. I wanted to understand the technology behind them.

When I started studying at Rizal College of Taal Inc., I realized that ACT is not just about playing with computers. It is about learning, solving problems, and helping people through technology. I am happy that I chose this course because every day I learn something new.

2. My First Networking Lab

Posted on October 2026 | By Ram Malabanan

One of my most memorable experiences as a 1st Year student was our first networking laboratory. We learned how to crimp a LAN cable using a crimping tool, RJ45 connector, and UTP cable.

At first, I was nervous because I was afraid to make a mistake. But when I tested my cable and the connection worked, I felt so proud. That experience taught me that patience and practice are important in networking.

3. Learning C# Programming

Posted on November 2026 | By Ram Malabanan

Learning C# was challenging for me at first. I had difficulty understanding loops, conditions, and variables. Sometimes my code had errors and I did not know how to fix them.

But I did not give up. I practiced, asked my classmates and teacher, and watched tutorials. Slowly, I started to understand. Now, I can create simple programs and I enjoy the feeling when my code finally runs without errors.

4. Life as a Student in Taal

Posted on November 2026 | By Ram Malabanan

Every day, I travel from Lemery to Taal to study at Rizal College of Taal Inc. It is tiring sometimes, but it is worth it because I meet new friends and learn new lessons.

Being a student in Taal taught me to be responsible and to manage my time. I learned to balance my studies, family, and hobbies. My goal is to finish my studies and make my family proud.
